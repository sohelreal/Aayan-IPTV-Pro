Learn More About Aayan IPTV Pro

Built specifically for smooth remote-control navigation and low-memory performance on Roku TVs and Roku streaming players.

Xtream Codes & M3U Support

Sign in quickly using your Xtream Codes Server URL, Username, and Password, or load any standard M3U / M3U8 playlist link. Includes a built-in Demo Mode for instant testing.

Live TV Split-Screen Preview

Browse categories and channels on the left while watching a live preview on the right. Channel #1 auto-plays when you open a category, and pressing OK again expands to full screen.

Movies & Series Library

Browse high-resolution posters, view director, release date, genre, and plot details, switch seasons, and play episodes directly from the Series Information screen.

Resume Playback & Favorites

Automatically saves your playback position for movies and series episodes, and keeps your favorite channels, movies, and series stored locally on your Roku TV.

Instant Search & Sorting

Filter folders in real time as you type or search inside all folders across your catalog. Sort lists by Default, A–Z, Z–A, Top Added, or Channel Number (ASC / DESC).

Customizable Settings

Switch between HLS (.m3u8) and MPEGTS (.ts) stream formats, choose 12-hour or 24-hour time display, and manage parental controls right from the Settings menu.
