Designed specifically for smooth remote-control navigation and low-memory performance on Roku TVs.

Xtream Codes & M3U Support

Log in quickly using your Xtream Codes Server URL, Username, and Password, or load any standard M3U / M3U8 playlist link. Includes a built-in Demo Mode for instant testing.

Live TV Split-Screen Preview

Browse categories and channels on the left while watching a live preview on the right. Press OK again on the playing channel to expand seamlessly to full screen.

Movies & Series Library

Browse high-resolution movie and series posters, read plot details and metadata, switch seasons, and select episodes directly from the Series Information screen.

Resume Playback & Favorites

Automatically remembers your exact playback position for movies and series episodes, and saves your favorite channels, movies, and series locally on your Roku TV.

Instant Search & Sorting

Filter folders as you type or search across your entire catalog. Sort lists by Default, A–Z, Z–A, Top Added, or Channel Number (Ascending / Descending).

Customizable TV Settings

Choose between HLS (.m3u8) and MPEGTS (.ts) stream formats, 12-hour or 24-hour clock display, and parental controls directly from the Settings menu.

Quick Setup & Remote Control Guide

  • Sign In: Enter your Xtream Codes Server URL, Username, and Password on the Login screen, or choose Load M3U URL / Try Demo Mode.
  • Live TV Full Screen: Select any channel to play in the preview window, then press OK a second time to expand to full screen.
  • Search, Sort & Favorites: Press the Star (*) button on your Roku remote in Live TV, Movies, or Series to search inside all folders, sort lists, or add/remove favorites.
  • Series Episodes: On the Series Information screen, use Left / Right to switch between Watch Now, Season, and Favorite, and press Down to browse and play episodes.
